WHAT IS NETA?

NETA is a scarce community-distributed digital asset on Juno. Its story is not about endless issuance. It is about ownership, transparency and what a small fixed supply can represent when it is held by a community.

> WHY THIS SITE EXISTS_

We want to make NETA's distribution visible. The ranking shows where the token sits today across Juno and Osmosis, lets every holder check their position, and creates a transparent public view of the asset's ownership structure.

THE IDEA

MORE THAN A TOKEN.

A COMMUNITY ASSET.

The long-term idea behind NETA is simple: a scarce asset can become more useful when it sits at the center of real activity. Liquidity, applications and community initiatives can all use the same neutral asset without changing its underlying scarcity.

That is why this project starts with something fundamental: making the ownership structure understandable and verifiable.

METHODOLOGY

WHAT THE DATA CURRENTLY MEANS

The ranking combines direct NETA balances on Juno and Osmosis. Identical 20-byte Juno/Osmosis wallet payloads are merged, and the Juno ICS20 escrow backing Osmosis NETA is excluded to prevent double counting.

Economic ownership: validated NETA DAO staking, unstaking and claimable positions are attributed to their individual wallets. Supported WYND and Osmosis liquidity positions are also redistributed proportionally to their economic owners. Bridge escrow, staking custody and pool custody are excluded to prevent double counting.
