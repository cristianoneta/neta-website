ⓘ METHODOLOGY v2

The ranking is generated from validated on-chain data for Juno, Osmosis and the NETA DAO.

Identical 20-byte Juno/Osmosis wallet payloads are merged. The Juno ICS20 escrow backing Osmosis NETA is excluded to prevent double counting.

DAO attribution: Active stakes, claims still in their release period and matured but unclaimed withdrawals are separated and assigned to their underlying Juno wallets.

LP attribution: NETA in the WYND JUNO/NETA pool and Osmosis Pool 631 is removed from pool custody addresses and distributed to economic LP owners, including staked and locked LP shares. Exact supply and reserve reconciliation is required.
